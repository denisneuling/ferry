ferry's code is licensed under the Apache License 2.0 ([LICENSE](LICENSE)).
As section 6 of that licence states, it does **not** grant permission to use
the metapatrol name, the metapatrol mark or wordmark, or the ferry lockups
built from them ("the marks").

The files in [`.metapatrol/`](.metapatrol) show the marks. They are generated
from `@metapatrol/brand` and are not under the Apache License; how they may be
used is described in that package and in the brand's TRADEMARKS document. In
short:

- **Without asking:** refer to ferry or metapatrol with the marks unchanged —
  in articles, talks, documentation, a list of tools you use — and keep them in
  a fork while it refers to this project.
- **With permission only:** use the marks for something that is not a
  metapatrol project (a fork distributed under its own name included), change
  them, or suggest that metapatrol endorses something it does not.

A fork distributed under its own name replaces `.metapatrol/` with its own.

The interface's own look ("Hafen") and its fonts are not marks: Fraunces and
DM Sans are under the SIL Open Font License, which travels with them.
